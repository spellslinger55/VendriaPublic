### History
In the early times, the lands of Khez were a land at constant war. Between smalls packs of Varkolaks, chiefdoms of Orcs, warbands of Goblinoids and other miscellaneous groups, blood flew freely in Khez. This, along with disastrous magics and demonic invasions that plagued the land caused the people of Khez into a secluded, isolationist lifestyle. It was only when the armies of the Empire of Astros came to Khez's borders that khezians had to face their neighboring mortals.
The plains of Khez were invaded and not so easily conquered. Even though they were in their early metallurgical era, khezian culture had a strong emphasis of martial prowess and so the people kept armies of the Empire at bay for longer than their enemies expected. Even so, with time they fell. Khez was conquered. And due to the not so fertile land and distance from the heart of the Empire to Khez, the lands became the center of research and experiments the Empire wanted to keep secret.
The people of Khez were accepted their new rulers and rebellions, uprisings and insurrections were a common happenstance across the plains. Even so, it was not until the fall of the Empire that Khezians were freed from the yoke of their oppressors. It was during those early years of the fall that the bloodiest rebellion took place, costing many, some say too many, lives across both sides of the conflict. But with no chains around their necks and knowledge they gained throughout years of servitude, Khezians quickly rebuilt what was left of themselves into a new, free civilization. They welcomed in any outsiders who wished for a new life and were willing to help build a society devoid of Astrian influence. The biggest achievement of Khez at this time was the construction of a school that came to be called the Free Academy of Rovlin, which to this day is known as the greatest school of science and magic. Even though Khezians had no interest in a ruling body and were doing perfectly fine as a social-anarchist society, the growth of others nations across the world and tension with them caused the Free Academy to be declared as a government of Khez, with its greatest wizard being called the Grandmaster Wizard-King of Khez. Even so, the main role of this body was not to govern Khez and its people but protect them from outside influences and pressure.
### Culture
Khez is a land prominent for magical advances, personal freedom, travelling lawman-vigilantes, hatred against slavery, and self-ruled settlements.
### Religion
The Thaumocracy has no official pantheon, allowing its citizens full freedom of religion. Religious Khezians tend to carry a fondness of rebellious gods like the Anarchist or Tagara, and gods of magic and knowledge like the Archivist or Mehit.

### Geography
Khez is located on the northernmost lands of the Astrian continent. The Thaumocracy mostly consists of vast, warm plains, low shrublands and dry savannas. Great forests spread in the south and west of the nation. Besides a single mountain range acting as its southern border it is mostly flat, with a few depressions and valleys here and there. It borders Bergov to the south and Nael to the east.
