### History
Before there was a Bergovian Kingdom, there was ancient kingdom of dwarves, its name forgotten to history. Said kingdom was in an eternal conflict with their elven neighbors, causing the two peoples to focus their military strength on each other. This was not missed by the humans living in the region. Their settlements, and powers, get growing undisturbed by their more powerful neighbors, until the human forces pushed an offensive into the heart of the Dwarven Kingdom. After defeating and forcing the dwarves to capitulate, the humans did not stop and instead pushed further into the deep forests of the elves, until they too, were conquered. With a peace treaty signed by the leaders of the three nations, the first Bergovian Kingdom was formed.
But a new, bigger nation and a new bigger name did not stop the wants of nobility: more resources to grow, more power to gather. And thus Bergov turned their spears south, through one their defensible mountain paths, into the fertile grasslands and forests. Thus they entered a border conflict with the city-states of the Commonwealth of Astros, who claimed these lands.
The conflict ended when a military coup transformed the Commonwealth into a budding Empire and the nobles of Bergov, swayed by positions of grandeur, betrayed their Kingdom and helped the armies of Astros march upon the bergovian lands.

After the fall of the Empire, many old noble families fought over the now empty throne of their newborn kingdom. Their long, indecisive infighting was interrupted when a simple peasant claimed to have been blessed by Saints and Gods of Bergov. With support from both the Church and peasants, the so called Saint-King rose above the nobles and claimed the throne. And thus was born the Divine Kingdom of Bergov, now under the rule of the Gryphonheart Dynasty.
### Culture
Bergov is a simple land of simple people. Peasants toil in the ground, chivalrous knights fight in tournaments, fey steal children in the night and maidens are saved from towers of mad wizards. 
### Religion
Bergov has firmly adopted the Astrian Pantheon, with the Harvester, the Rose and the Hangman being the most popular deities. Unlike other Astrianist nations, Bergov has a strong faith of the Saints and is famed for extravagant churches in their names. The most famous of these are the:
- _the Hallowed Church in the name of His Passage_
- _the Church of Saint Antonia the Resolute_.
### Geography
Bergov is located in central Astros. The land bound kingdom neighbors Khez to the north, Nael to the east and wild No Man's Lands to the west and south. Bergovian landscape consists primarily of dense forests, low hills, with the occasional lakes and rivers. Sprinkled throughout the land one could find ancient obelisks, groves, spires and other objects sacred to druidic sects. The land of Bergov is situated between two massive mountain ranges, the north one acting as a natural border between Bergov and Khez.
