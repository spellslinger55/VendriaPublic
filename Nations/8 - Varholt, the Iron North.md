### History
According to Varrian legends, the snowy realm was originally a territory of the fabled giants, the Gangrandr, who were said to have shaped the world of Vendria when it was raw. Said giants supposedly departed the lands and the world itself after the demise of their even more ancient gods. Whether the stories are true or not is fought over, but either way. in the absence of the Gangrandr, lands of Varholt came to be inhabited by the fearsome Vurms, undead draconic creatures, who took control of the frozen lands and the mortals therein. The tyranny of Vurms lasted for hundreds of years, until four mortal heroes, today known as the Great Progenitors, unearthed ancient artifacts and magics hidden in their land. Together, they gathered the mortals of Varholt and unified them under a single banner to fight against their oppression, an event that would come to be known as _The Vurm Slaying_.  
In their defeat, the Vurms cursed the lands of Varholt with dark magic, summoning vile monstrosities and infusing the land itself with necromantic energies, dooming the peaceful future people of Varholt wished for. Despite the curses now plaguing Varholt, the four tribes that were formed by the Great Progenitors decided to remain in Varholt and together focused their new might on cleansing the blights put upon their nation.  
The first outsiders Varrians came into contact with were travelers from Parll, their islander neighbors that Varrians came to considered cousins. Despite their varying believes and practices, Pallrians and Varrians remained on friendly terms for a long time, even after Hirana took control of the island.  
When the Empire of Astros came to the shores of Varholt in an attempt to conquer the lands, they were met with mighty resistance from the natives, unified once more under a single banner against a foreign oppressor.
### Culture
The lands of Varholt are prominent for their nomadic tribes, horrors which stalk the night, great feasts and ceremonies, and hardened, wild warriors.  
While every tribe is ruled by a Elkhan (chieftain), one among them is always titled a Hakan (chief of chiefs) in the case of a inter-tribe disaster, such as an invasion from another nation or the return of old enemies.  
The people of Varholt are split into four tribes, each named after one of the Great Progenitors:
- the Stanayit Tribe, named after the great slayer and warrior, who reside in central Varholt
- the Almr Tribe, named after the great tamer and rider, who reside among the mountains and ice deserts of Varholt
- the Vanadi Tribe, named after the great mystic and herbalist, who reside in the great forests of Varholt
- the Sivrit Tribe, named after the great craftsman and inventor, who reside along the coasts of Varholt
### Religion
Varrians believe that the gods worshiped by other cultures are undeserving of the title of gods and that the true deities of Vendria have died a long time ago ago. Instead of gods, Varrians celebrate and hold ceremonies for the their deceased ancestors.  
### Geography
Varholt is part on the Saarian continent, encompassing its northernmost reaches. The North Pole of Vendria resides in Varholt. The land neighbors Shayin and Hirana through No Man's Lands and untamed waters. The region is full of tundras, taigas, ice and snow deserts, as well as wild and frozen wastelands. The highest mountains in Vendria are located in this region.
