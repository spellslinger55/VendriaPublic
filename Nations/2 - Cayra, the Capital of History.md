### History
The Commonwealth of Astros was a nation built by multiple city-states, and their Philosopher Kings, joining hands to rally against monsters at their walls. While the union served them well for years, troubles began when Astros came into border conflict with neighboring nations. Deemed too passive and weak by the military personel at the time, the General of one of the city-states performed a coup and placed himself in a position of power as the First Emperor of the Empire of Astros.
Cayra was established by the First Emperor as the Throne City of the Empire during the very first years of his conquest. The city took many years to build but was called the greatest, most grandiose city of the world, befitting the name of the Crown Jewel of the Empire. The Empire's historians also dubbed the Capital of the World, for it was going to be the center of the Empire, and the whole world would be the Empire. 
As the Empire kept growing, so did the city. With each day, it expanded. New people, new districts, new buildings, the cycle repeating. Cayra grew from one city into many cities build atop one another.  
After the fall of the Empire, Cayra survived. It became a self-regulated city-state lead by a Princeps (who was previously a city official tasked with running the city) and their Privy Council (representatives of the seven most powerful noble families). Few of the Empire's old Noble Houses  survived alongside the city and even fewer managed to stay in power for long. With time, their positions weakened as they clung to old traditions and refused to change. Economical issues born from the fall of the Empire lead to the creation and unionizing of many Guilds, consisting of low and middle class workers, craftsmen and merchants, whose positions rose into prominence.
### Culture
Cayra is a land of endless possibilities, cultural mishmashes, and new idea in the worlds of science, art and magic. Members of the old Noble Houses still cling to the ideologies of the Empire, promoting classism, misogyny and xenophobia in their circles.  
The Privy Council currently consists of the three Major Guilds and four Major Noble Houses.
The Major Guilds include:
- *Naviculariorum*, the merchant's guild,
- *Avisa*, the crier's guild,
- *Farriers Association*, the commuter's guild
Lesser Guilds include: Blood & Darkness, a gladiator's guild; the Pocket, a tailor's guild; White Cloth Guild, a custodian's guild.  
The Major Noble Houses include:
- *House Valerius*, commanding His Majesty's Armada,
- *House Augustus*, commanding the Imperial University,
- *House Cygnus*, commanding the Exalted Temple,
- *House Tarius*, commanding the Royal Treausery
Some of the Minor Houses include: House Sangallo, House Lagraulas, House Delacroix.
### Religion
While in the past the Astrian Pantheon was the most prominent religion in the city, with time and influx of people, all religions, big or small, are represented in this grand city.
### Geography
Cayra is located on the most southern part of the Astrian continent. It is a huge, urban conglomeration with limited space for parks and farmlands within its borders. It borders Nael by the Wine Red Sea. Below the street and the vast sewer system of Cayra, large excavations of cave systems took place, allowing for creation of a second city under the city for those less inclined to live in the light.
