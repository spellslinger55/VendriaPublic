### History
The Archipelago of Qan has always been the most secluded nation, thanks to not only their geographical position making it hard to reach for others, but also thanks to the native dangers of the archipelago, including but not limited to active volcanoes, man-eating plans and great terror lizards. Early Qan was a home to many tribes of reptilian mortals who lived in shaky harmony. It was not until the powerful push of the Nukomikarangi, the Snake Tribe, to conquer each and every piece of land within the archipelago. The cause of this sudden change and push is unknown, but many blame the insanity of their serpentine deities.
The Nukomikarangi Dominion conquered the archipelago, killing their reptilian cousins and enslaving those deemed worthy of servitude. With their forces, Nukomikarangi built great cities of gold and gemstones nestled deep within the qanese jungles. But even that was not enough for their foul, ophidian deities and soon the black sciences of alchemy and magecraft was used to build even greater monuments. And when their current workforce was not enough, the Nukomikarangi used their sciences to artificially created plant-based humanoids to fuel their labor. The plan was successful at first and the Dominion entered a brief Golden Age but as their deities silenced, the other mortals of Qan rebelled in full force against their overlords, beginning the downfall of the Nukomikarangi Dominion.
After grueling years of warfare and insurrection, one day, the Nukomikarangi simply vanished without a trace, leaving their grand cities empty. The mortals of Qan, poisoned through years of slavery and whispers of foul deities, turned against each other, each believing they deserved more inheritance from their would-be-captors. It was only when hiranian and astrian explorers came to the shores of Qan that people of Qan quickly ended their civil war and once more took up weapons against shared opponents.
After the fall of the Empire, Qan officially ceased all wartime efforts, against other nations and itself alike, and joined hands to form a Union than could withstand any new enemy forces. Even so, whispers of rebellion and conflict spread through the islands.
### Culture
Qan is a land prominent for their hunter-gatherer societies, great and terrible beasts, ostentatious cities of gold and gemstones and ruins hidden within the jungles.
### Religion
People of Qan follow the Qanese Pantheon. Humans primarily follow the Gold and Lightning Sakas, quetzpalin the Blood and Sweat Sakas and oran the Vortex and Root Sakas, but none are limited to just those.  
### Geography
Qan is an archipelago consisting of five major islands and multiple smaller ones surrounding them. Most of the islands are humid, gigantic, overgrown jungles populated by divers fauna and flora, with certain islands sporting massive sandy beaches and even multiple volcanoes, some active.  
The Amber Island is inhabited by humans, the Bloodfell Island by quetzpalin, King's Demise by oran and the Kentron Island split between all three. Remnant of Peace is a neutral island, inhabited by all as a sign of peace between the people of Qan. It is also the only island through which foreigners may enter the archipelago.
