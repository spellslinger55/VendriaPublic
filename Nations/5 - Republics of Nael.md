### History
The people of early Nael were adverse to writing and their culture, religion and stories were spread only through the spoken word. What little knowledge survived of those mortals after the conquest of Nael by the Empire of Astros can only be found in Naelian poems, plays and songs. 
The lands of Nael were favoured by nobles of the Empire. Even though they were rich in resources, Nael was more famed for its beautiful views and pleasant weathers. Astrian officials bought up any pieces of the land from the Empire as they could, splitting Nael into smaller provinces, each with its own rules. In some, Naelians were treated no better than beasts of burden, but in many they instead served Astrians as fools, playwrights, buskers and actors, allowed to spread the culture of Nael through seemingly innocent art. In the provinces were oppression was light, Naelian who served Astrians were even allowed to study and learn the arts of navigation, shipwrighting, astronomy and the like. It was not unheard of for a Naelian to be granted an official position within the Empire. 
Once the Empire of Astros fell, the provinces of Nael banded together. Due to politics, societal and economic issues, five republias were formed within Nael, the leaders of which, known as Ducas, formed a Council that now governed the new birthed Republics of Nael. With their newfound freedom the people of Nael continued the mastering of arts and sciences brought forth from the Empire, mainly focusing on establishing a powerful economy. In the wake of this new civilization, three powerful merchant companies, now known throughout the lands of Vendria, came into power, their riches and connections allowing for manipulating Nael from behind the scenes.
### Culture
Nael is a land prominent for their free-spirited, stubborn, adventurous explorers, amazing sailors, wine, rich theatrical works, and great merchant companies. Naelian art, religion and philosophy is heavily influenced by other cultures, as their sailors and travelers bring many ideas back to Nael.  
The three merchant companies that rule Nael from the shadows are:
- Silver Tongue, famed for their skilled couriers
- La Serenissima, famed for their export of Naelian goods
- Crux, famed for their ships reaching any and every port in the world
### Religion
Nael has firmly adopted the Astrian Pantheon, primarily focusing their faith on the Stargazer, the Author and the Traveller. Thanks to the culture of travel among Naelians and lots of contact with people of other cultures and religions, it is common to find followers, shrines, churches and works of art of different Pantheons in the Republics.
### Geography
Nael is located on the eastern reaches of the Astrian continent. It borders Bergov, Cayra and Khez to the west and two seas: the Wine Red Sea to the south and the Grakkian Sea to the east. Naelian land mainly consists of vast, green plains and grasslands with vineyards and forests sprinkled throughout the countryside. The five Republias of Nael are: Ovest, Isole, Fa-la-Mer, Insulinda and La Melina.
