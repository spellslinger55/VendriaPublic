### History
According to legends, the lands today known as Shayin belonged to an ancient civilization of people known to be the first followers of the Saarian Pantheon. That is until they betrayed the gods and served their immortal enemies, the Twin Beasts. As punishment, maelstroms of magic and dust tore through the ancient civilization, ripping the souls and bodies of its people, and turning the lands into a arid desert.  
The early Shanese civilization settled on these treacherous deserts, only thanks to their knowledge on how to foretell and counter the disastrous weather of the region, which protected them against enemies. Early on, after the nation was stabilized, Shayin came to be ruled by a man now known only as the Chakravat, the God-King, the Avatar of Malik, who lead the people for hundreds of years, apparently capable of controlling the sands of time around Shayin, easily able to manipulate the populace of Shayin for his nefarious deeds. His eternal rule came to an end when hiranian outsiders came to Shayin, finally revealing the Fallen King's deeds against his subjects.  
After overthrowing the Chakravat, a new, noble government, were each caste of Shayin was represented and had a say in the future of the people and country. After stability returned to Shayin, the new first Sar-Sarrani, the King of Kings and ruler of Shayin, declared war on Hirana after failure to form peaceful diplomatic relations. Said war remained in effect until a ceasefire was called when the Empire of Astros came to the shores of the eastern continent. For the first time in hundreds of years, the people of Shayin and Hirana were not at war. However, peace lasted only for as long as the Empire did, as Hirana invaded the borders of Shayin a scant couple of years after the fall of the Empire.
### Culture
Shayin is a land prominent for their few but enormous cities, caste system, ancient secrets hidden beneath the magical sands and storms.  
The Castes of Shayin include:  
- Sarrani (the Royalty), which includes the royal family and all nobility
- An-Ki (the Intelligentsia), which includes scholars, academics, writers, philosophers, and clergy
- Enwa (the Warriors), which includes soldiers, guards, and mercenaries
- Cunei (the Commoners), which includes the merchants, farmers, and craftsmen
- Kuara (the Servants), which includes foreigners and exiles
### Religion
Shayin firmly follows the Saarian Pantheon, the most popular deities being the King, the Rivers and the Flute, as well as their epithets. The biggest temple in Shayin is also an astronomical observatory that houses a library of the most accurate star and world maps produced in Vendria.
### Geography
Shayin is located on the western reaches of the Saarian continent. It borders Hirana to the east and Varholt through the sea to the north. Lands of Shayin consist mainly of vast, sandy deserts with a few oases, valleys and mountains sprinkled in-between. Some rock deserts can be found as well. In the northwest of the nation resides an enormous bay filled with infernal beasts and dangerous monstrosities, called the Bay of Solomon.
