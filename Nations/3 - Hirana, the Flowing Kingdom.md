### History
The early Kingdom of Hirana was formed when either the dragonborn or elvish people, historians actively argue which, began a diplomatic journey through the lands to gather mortals and form a unified front against the horrors of the world. While their start was weak and slow, with time, the people of Hirana formed a powerful nation, though along the way their peaceful ways fell to the wayside as some of the lands were taken by force or intimidation, like the island of Pallr.
Ever since the conception of the kingdom, while the Lordship held most power in the nation, they were kept in check and balance by the Six Great Clans that served them but also controlled some provinces of the land. And even though Clans fell and were replaced throughout the times, the might of the Lordship over the lands remained.  
After Hirana stabilized as a kingdom and rebellions and bandits were suppressed, scouts, diplomats and campaigners were sent away beyond the borders in searches of fortune, glory and fertile land. It was in campaigns that Hirana made contact with their eastern neighbors, which began their endless conflict over borders and resources. Similarly, the hiranians were the first people to make contact with the southern nation of Qan, which ended with the two at conflict. It was during a skirmish on an island of Qan, that hiranian forces captured scouts of the Empire of Astros and first made contact with the juggernaut of a nation. With the contact made, dignitaries of the Empire, Shayin and even Varholt met on the lands of Hirana. While the initial contact seemed to be peaceful, the Empire soon launched an invasion on the eastern lands. A ceasefire was called between Hirana and their neighbors, while the military focus became the western conquerors. 
But when the Empire fell peace was short as Shayin and Hirana returned to a cold war once more.
### Culture
Hirana is a feudal land, prominent for their wandering warriors, practitioners of ancient arts, vast underground cities, and sagely philosophers.  
The current Six Great Clans are:
- Crane Clan, lead by the Yoshida family
- Fox Clan, lead by the Itou family
- Turtle Clan, lead by the Minnsun family
- Hydra Clan, lead by the Naaji family
- Basilisk Clan, lead by the Xun family
- Chimera Clan, lead by the Samar family
### Religion
Hirana follows the Saarian Pantheon, the most popular deities being the King, the Crow and the Dragon, as well as their epithets.
### Geography
Hirana is located on the eastern reaches of the Saarian continent. To the west it borders Shayin and No Man's Land by the name of the Plains of Fear to the north. The lands of Hirana are filled by plains and shrublands as well as many rivers, marches and swamps. Multiple geysers and hot spring areas can be located in the north-east as well as on the island of Pallr. Located under Hirana are huge caverns and cave systems where multiple underground cities have been built. To the north Hirana borders the Twisting and the Fallgrave Ocean to the east
