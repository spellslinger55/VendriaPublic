## Basic information
#### Anatomy
Moddotti resemble furry humanoids of short stature, with pointed snouts, small rounded ears and scaly tails (often compared to mice, rats, jerboas, shrews).
A fascinating quirk of moddotti biology is a specialized pouch within their stomachs that absorbs nutrients and magical energy as they grow, causing each moddotti to alchemically mutate with age.
#### Habitats
Temperate, subtropical steppes and grasslands.
#### Diet
Omnivorous, but primarily herbivorous.
#### Growth rate
Moddotti mature at a faster rate than humans and reach adulthood in their early teens.
## Civilization
#### History
Moddottis are the descendants of the first settlers of mainland Nael. Like their ancestors, they avoided confrontations with other, more powerful civilizations and survived through the use of stealth, cleverness and coordination. 
#### Historical Organizations
- Department of Modern Alchemy - one of the major departments of the Free Academy of Rovlin, which focuses their research on subjects like alchemy, transmutation, chemistry, herbology and medicine
- the Liners - the first legal Thieve's Guild of Nael, hired to probe the defenses and security of objects of interest in order to strengthen them against real attacks
- Silver Tongue - one of the Three Naelian Merchant Companies
#### Common Taboos
- Hoarding
- Ostentatious living (i.e. jewelry, expensive clothing, large houses)
#### Historical figures
- Sir Cyryl the Grey - greatest Witch Hunter of the Order of the Radiant Pyre, one of the Hangman's Warrior-Priest Orders
- Olya Greenfeet - staunch opponent of Naelian capitalist tendencies and mother of olyanism (vendrian anarcho-communism)
- the Rat King - creator and enigmatic leader of the biggest khezian crime syndicate
