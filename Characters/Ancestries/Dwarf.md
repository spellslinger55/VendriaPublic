## Basic information
#### Anatomy
Dwarves resemble humanoids of short stature, with wide bodies, short limbs and large projecting noses. Dwarven skeletons, especially their skulls, are much tougher than those of most humanoids with traces of stones and metals in them. On average, dwarven hair grows faster, longer and from a younger age than in other species.  
Hill dwarves tend to be wider than their cousins, with broader shoulders and foreheads and commonly with darker skin and hair.  
Mountain dwarves tend to be leaner than their cousins, and commonly with paler skin and brighter hair.  
Cave dwarves tend to be taller than their cousins, and have skin in pale grey and blue shades (resembling argyria), with pale colored hair.
#### Habitats
Polar and temperate mountains and caves.
#### Diet
Omnivorous.
#### Growth rate
Dwarves mature at the same rate as humans, but they're considered young in dwarven culture until they reach the age of 50. On average, they live about 200 years.  
## Civilization
#### History
Not much is known about the origins of dwarves, or how their people have spread throughout the lands of Vendria. 
In ancient Bergov, a dwarven kingdom at war with elves was ambushed and conquered by humans, which later formed the Bergovian Kingdom.
In ancient Hirana, the dwarves formed a union with the underground dwelling dusk elves and together sculpted vast, underground cities, which later joined the Kingdom of Hirana.
In ancient Varholt, it is said that the cunning dwarves mistrusted and fought the giant Gangrandr, who then ruled the frozen lands. When they vanished and the tyranny of Vurms spread, the dwarven jarldoms joined forces with other mortals of Varholt to oppose the draconic despots.
#### Historical Organizations
- The Stone Sentry - an ancient, bergovian order of warrior-druids who guarded the mountain realms of Bergov
- Blood & Darkness, the Gladiator's Guild - greatest of the gladiator's guilds in Cayra, formed when the Bloody Spectacle Guild and the Dark Entertainment Guild merged and combined their wealth
- the Hatar Brewing Company - the richest, most known producers of alcohol in Hirana
#### Common Taboos
- Cowardice in the face of opponents greater than yourself 
- Poor craftsmanship, better do nothing than do a bad job
- Badly maintained facial hair (shaving is ok, it just has to be clean and on point)
#### Historical figures
- Sivrit the Ironguard - progenitor of the Sivrit Tribe and the greatest inventor, smith and craftsman to grace Varholt
- Ardin Xun - most famous member of the Basilisk Clan, who gave his life to close a portal to the Abyss. Some say he still fights back the demonic hordes in the Abyssal Wastelands
- Bradrod Trollmace - current general of the Bergovian Army, a Drakkon of the previous king
