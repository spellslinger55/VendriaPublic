## Basic information
#### Anatomy
Oran resemble humanoids with reptilian and floral features. Shorter than average humanoids, the oran have skin in shades of green, usually more vibrant if their diet consists of apt amounts of drinking water. Their ears are short and pointed and their vibrantly colored eyes resemble those of snakes. Instead of hair their heads sprout foliage, flowers, vines, moss, etc., that are often stylized in a way resembling hair. Oran are sexually monomorphic with flat chests, narrow shoulders and broad hips. They possess the ability to extend their circulatory system outside of their body and use it to absorb liquids, which they can also do through their skin. Oran blood is orange in color, glossy like sap, and smells sweet.
#### Habitats
Tropical forests and grasslands.
#### Diet
Omnivorous, but primarily hydrovorous.
#### Growth rate
Oran mature at the same rate humans do and reach adulthood in their late teens, but some of them never stop physically growing.
## Civilization
#### History
Oran history begins with mad priest-scientists of the Nukomikarangi Dominion, who experimented with plant-matter. Through foul alchemy, dark magics and blood rituals, the snakes created oran as an efficient and eternal workforce. Though the first generation was malleable and recipient to nukomikarangi slavery, the second generation of oran to be born were quick to rebel against their overlords.
#### Historical Organizations
- Apexworks - the biggest trading company within Qan who oversees 90% of trade between the archipelago and other nations
- the Gnarled Roots - insurgents who rejects the peace treaty of Qan and actively sabotages attempts of continued cooperation, primarily by kidnapping and/or killing important humans and quetzpalin
- Blossomkeepers - socio-political movement that eschews modern technology and the reliance on nukomikarangi cities and inheritance, and calls for a true and pure return to the origin of tribal Qan
#### Common Taboos
- Slavery
- Eugenics, transmutations and genetic alchemy
- Cremation
#### Historical figures
- Kalani the First Rebel - believed to be the first of the oran ancestry to stand against the Nukomikarangi Dominion and slay one of the snakes with naught but her roots
- Leai of the Scarlet Petal - greatest oran weaponsmith and master of the esoteric Living Steel
- High Priestess Mayyahuel - current leader of the oran people and the Twofold Avatar of Vortex and Root, whose left hand is freedom and right hand is death

# Oran
**Ability Score Increase.** +1 Intelligence, +1 Wisdom  
**Size.** Medium  
**Speed.** 30ft  
**Hydrovore.** You don’t need to eat and can fully sustain yourself on water.  
**Lull.** Magic can't put you to sleep. You can finish a long rest in 6 hours if you spend those hours in an inactive, motionless state. During this time, you appear dormant but remain conscious.  
**Darkvision.** You can see in dim light within 60 feet of you as if it were bright light, and in darkness as if it were dim light. With this vision, you see color based on heat.  
**Taking Root.** When you use your Unarmed Strike, you can choose to attack with your roots. You can add your Constitution modifier, instead of your Strength modifier, to the attack and damage rolls when you attack this way. This attack deals 1d4 Necrotic damage. In addition, when you deal this damage to a creature that isn't a Construct or an Undead, you can empower yourself in one of the following ways:
- *Drink.* You regain Hit Points equal to the Necrotic damage dealt.
- *Strengthen.* You gain a bonus to the next ability check or attack roll you make within the next minute; the bonus is equal to the Necrotic damage dealt.
You can empower yourself with this trait a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.
