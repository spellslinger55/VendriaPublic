## Basic information
#### Anatomy
Yagas resemble tall, flightless, avian humanoids. Their legs are long, powerful and end with sharp talons, akin to raptors, giving them powerful jumping abilities. Their bodies are adorned with various feathers, usually forming vestigial wings, tails and even cloak-like plumage that lets yagas glide through the air. 
#### Habitats
Temperate forests and mountains.
#### Diet
Omnivorous.
#### Growth rate
Yagas age faster than humans and reach adulthood in their early teens.
## Civilization
#### History
Yagas occupied the western forests and mountain reaches of Bergov, sharing the region with early satyrs. As resources grew scarcer and the tribes expanded, the two ancestries came into conflict, which ended with satyrs being driven away by the yagas. Content with their firm control of the region, yagas did not expand but instead consolidated their powers and fortified their settlements. When the expansion of the Bergovian Kingdom reached their borders, yagas were invited into the kingdom.
#### Historical Organizations
- Royal Aerial Company - elite cavalry of the bergovian army, mounts of which consists fully of flying monsters, such as pegasi, hippogriffs, dragonnels, and similar
- Devil's Perch - enforcers and mercenaries allied with the Freewater Market, Bergov's primary black market scene
- Kir Savann - collectors who seek out evil and dangerous artifacts, with the goal of researching them to find a way of destroying them
