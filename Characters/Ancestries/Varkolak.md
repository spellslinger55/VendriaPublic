## Basic information

#### Anatomy
Varkolaks resemble massive humanoid with canine-like heads (often compared to dogs, wolves, hyenas). They are taller than average humanoids, with lean musculature. Their skin is usually in black and grey colors, with short fur in black, white, grey and red hues. The pattern on their fur is hereditary, but commonly difficult for non-Varkolaks to recognize. Some Varkolaks in addition to their full-body fur also grow a crest-like mane in dirty white, grey and reddish shades. They have large, blunt, non-retractable claws and sharp, bone-crushing fangs. Varkolaks are sexually dimorphic, with females on average being larger, with broader shoulders and waists, with thicker fur and longer tails.
#### Habitats
Temperate steppes and grasslands.
#### Diet
Omnivorous, but primarily carnivorous.
#### Growth rate
Varkolaks mature faster than humans do and reach adulthood by age 12.
## Civilization
#### History
Varkolaks were one of the original inhabitants of khezian plains, and albeit the least numbered of the mortals there, varkolak packs fought tooth and nail for the survival of their people. When famine and demons scoured the lands, varkolaks took drastic measures to ensure their survival, and were forced to consume the meat of their fallen enemies, mortals and fiends both. 
#### Historical Organizations
- White Cloth Guild - Cayra's primary Cleaner's Guild, responsible for keeping the streets of Cayra clean and trash-free
- Spawns of Anarchy - vagrants, vagabonds, hooligans who traverse the western continent on their trusty steads, spreading chaos wherever they go (aka Vendria's primary fantasy biker gang)
- Sundered Will - a military group that specializes in awakening and empowering the varkolak's innate bloodlust to strengthen themselves in battle, technique originally used by the Empire on their warrior-slaves
#### Common Taboos
- Betrayal, especially of family
- Desecration of the dead
- Consumption of alcohol
#### Historical figures
- Lady Vitya the Scarred - demonologist and traitor to her people, faithful servant of the Empire
- Packlord Praskoya - the last of the Packlords, died protecting varkolaks from the Empire
- Marcel Spotfur - legendary trickster who wanders the world, getting into trouble with his mischief. Protagonist of any cautionary children's tales
