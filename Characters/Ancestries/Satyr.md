## Basic information
#### Anatomy
Satyrs resemble bestial humanoids. All satyrs possess a short, scruffy tail, a pair of furry legs and cloven feet. Some possess more other animalistic features, such as: elongated, floppy ears; horizontal slit-shaped pupils; flat noses; more fur; etc..  
The heads of winter satyrs are adorned with antlers, like those of deer, moose, reindeer or the like.  
The heads of summer satyrs are adorned with horns, like those of goat, sheep, antelope or the like.
#### Habitats
Temperate forests and mountains.
#### Diet
Omnivorous, but primarily herbivorous.
#### Growth rate
Satyrs mature at the same rate humans do but reach adulthood in their early twenties.
## Civilization
#### History
The fay of Bergov had always been a menace, especially in the olden days. Satyrs experienced it first hand, when all their people wandered too far into a dense forest, escaping from battle with yagas, that transported them into the fay realms. As fey hospitality tends to do, the people were welcomed and never intended to let go. Through cleverness and trickery of their own, satyrs managed to escape their imprisonment, after years upon years of chaotic existence. What came back to the lands of Vendria were mortals forever changed and mutated by their magics of the fey realms.
#### Historical Organizations
- Thistlehooves - once the royal guards of the satyr tribes, now fallen on hard times with the unification of Bergov, named after the pale purple warpaint the tribe would adorn themselves with
- Royal Parade - cult dedicated to fey royalty, born from those who regretted returning to the mortal planes
- D.D.D.D. - prestigious and secret group that organizes the wildest, most sought after parties and festivities
#### Common Taboos
- Envy, greed and general vanity  
- Inhospitality
#### Historical figures
- Joachim Redhorn - founder of psychiatry, psychotherapy, and psychology, as well as staunch adherent and proponent of hedonism
- Arianhrod Calyn - great criminal and great wizard alike, who developed and mastered many of the spells used for mind control
- Eirian, the Green Scar - satyr warrior whose more than 50% of their body was replaced with living, elemental earth
