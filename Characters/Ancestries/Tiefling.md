## Basic information

#### Anatomy
Tieflings resemble their parentage in shape and size but the fiendish influence in their blood can be easily recognized. Every tiefling is born with horns sprouting from their head, most often with other, seemingly random mutations.  
Common mutations for Ash tieflings include: sharp fangs; claws; bestial eyes, ears, and tails; fur.  
Common mutations for Cinder tieflings include: cloven feet; vestigial, leathery wings; pointed ears or tongue; thin, leathery tails; solid colored eyes.  
Common mutations for Soot tieflings include: metallic horns, nails, and teeth; irregular horns (more horns, less horns, horns on one side of head, etc.); rotten stench; cold, corpse-like skin; bony or metallic growths.

## Civilization
#### History
Tieflings, unlike most other ancestries, are a people born from mutations to other ancestries, in this case induced by fiendish powers. These corruptions can be traced back to major event that touched all of Vendria, and small, personal dealing, like devil contracts or consuming demon flesh.
Cinder tieflings trace the fiendish influence in their blood to the devils of the Nine Hells. The first of these tieflings were born on the deserts of Shayin, due to Archmage Solomon's studies. Thanks to her standing and politics, tieflings face very little prosecution in the shanese lands.  
Ash tieflings trace the fiendish influence in their blood to the demons of the Abyss. The first of these tieflings were born on the plains of Khez, due to demonic incursions started by goblinoids. Tieflings, sometimes also called warborn by khezians, were considered cursed children whose only purpose in life was combat. While some of these superstition can still be found in Khez, tieflings are more common and accepted in modern Khez. 
Soot tieflings trace the fiendish influence in their blood to the chorts of Pandemonium. The first of these tieflings were born in the Empire of Astros, after astrian spellcasters made contact with chorts to procure their accursed armaments.
#### Historical Organizations
- Army of the Night - a secret organization of monster hunters, dedicated to silently removing creatures that infiltrate mortal societies (i.e. vampires, werewolves, doppelgangers, hags) without alerting the common populace
- the Absolution - a zealous group that preaches the innate corruption and filth of all tieflings, and that believes all tieflings have to spend their lives repenting for being born
- the Lost-Home - nomadic orphanage, originally started by tieflings banished from neighboring villages, who collect other exiles and undesirables and grants them a home, even if its only temporary
#### Common Taboos
- Touching horns in public
- Cutting / breaking off / getting rid of horns
#### Historical figures
- Jedidiah - first shanese tiefling, son of Solomon the Archmage, who rejected his mother's teachings and founded a rival school of magic, credited as the only person to ever master chronomancy
- Gavril Dupin - greatest cayran detective who has solved more than 200 cases, made more famous by the biographical book series written by his assistant 
- Merry Virion - one of the Five Sea Princes, pirate captain of the Dividers fleet, most bloodthirsty of all known pirates
