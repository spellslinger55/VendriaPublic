## Basic information

#### Anatomy
Orcs resemble bulky humanoids of imposing stature, with green-grey skin, and a powerful jaw equipped with a pair of elongated lower tusks, which protrude out of their mouths. Orcs are commonly far-sighted and their eyes appear to glow dimly in darkness. 
#### Habitats
Temperate and subtropical forests, steppes and grasslands.
#### Diet
Omnivorous.
#### Growth rate
Orcs age faster than humans and reach adulthood around the age of 14.
## Civilization
#### History
Orcs were one of the most common people among the plains of Khez. Despite not forming a singular kingom, the martial prowess of orcish clans and their tactical acumen was nothing to scoff at, and so orcs came to rule most of Khez when the Empire arrived.
Orcs, who were in the middle of their metallurgic age, were crushed and enslaved by the Empire, like most other peoples unwilling to bend the knee. The orcs remained indomitable and wild spirited the whole time, opposing the Empire until its fall and their last, successful rebellion.  
After the fall, orcs were the main force leading the rebirth of Khez as a free region.
#### Historical Organizations
- The Crucible - union of engineers and various craftsmen, Khez's primary caretakers of infrastructure, like roads, bridges, dams, tunnels, etc.
- Black Knights of Sol - group of anti-slavery vigilantes, formed by Sol, Saint of the Black Sun, when the Empire still ruled Khez
- the Twofold Clan - ancient order of battle-priests, that have held onto a traditional belief that children born on borders (a solstice, an eclipse, born as twins, etc.) are destined for glory
#### Common Taboos
- Slavery
- Giving up
- Eating alone
#### Historical figures
- Drazahn Kolovisk - father of drazahnism, Khez's primary political movement (vendrian social-democracy)
- Murgen the First - primary leader of the Last Khezian Rebellion, which lead to the reformation of Khez as a free nation and removing the last powers of the Empire from its lands, at the cost of many, many lives
- Varg the Hunter - bounty hunter, most famous for the 8 foot tall greatbow he uses

# Orc
**Ability Score Increase.** +2 Strength  
**Size.** Medium  
**Speed.** 30ft  
**Darkvision.** You can see in dim light within 60ft of you as if it were bright light, and in darkness as if it were dim light. You can't discern color in darkness, only shades of gray.  
**Powerful Build.** You have Advantage on any saving throw you make to end the Grappled condition on yourself. Moreover, you count as one size larger when determining your carrying capacity and the weight you can push, drag or lift.  
**Old Traditions.** You have proficiency in one of the following skills of your choice: Animal Handling, Nature, Survival, Persuasion, Intimidation.  
**Relentless Endurance.** When you are reduced to 0 Hit Points but not killed outright, you can drop to 1 Hit Point instead. You can't use this feature again until you finish a Long Rest.
