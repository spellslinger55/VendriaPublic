## Basic information
#### Anatomy
Halflings resemble humanoids of short stature with large amounts of body hair. Commonly, they tend to have round features (cheeks, ears, eyes, etc.) and big feet with leathery soles resistant to damage.  
#### Habitats
Any.
#### Diet
Extremely omnivorous.
#### Growth rate
Halflings mature at the same rate humans do and reach adulthood in their late teens but they tend to live longer.
## Civilization
#### History
Not much is known about halfling history, even to halflings themselves. What is known, is that they never formed a civilization of their own and were instead first recorded migrating to settlements and villages of other residents of Nael.
#### Historical Organizations
- Ramriders - order of bergovian knights, famous for mounting not horses but vicious combat rams
- The Vulgar Crab - one of the most popular taverns in all of Cayra
- the Lost Library - an esoteric and mysterious group, with no known headquarters, said to possess all books and art that has ever been lost and not found
#### Common Taboos
- Inhospitality. Let the weary traveler rest their feet, share your water with them
- Stealing from other halflings. We're all family
#### Historical figures
- Marcelino Maria Niccolo Raul Abbandonato - founder and current head of Nael's Abbandonato Crime Family
- Yamyra Appletopple - graveknight of Ershetu, Pestilence Incarnate, the Archdevil of Bones 
- Ealswid Hogbloom - legendary gentleman thief of Naelian high-society

# Halfling
**Ability Score Increase.** +2 Dexterity  
**Size.** Small  
**Speed.** 25ft  
**Language of the People.** You can speak, read and write Halfling.  
**Lucky.** When you roll a 1 on the d20 for an attack roll, ability check, or saving throw, you can reroll the die and must use the new roll.  
**Halfling Nimbleness.** You can move through the space of any creature that is of a size larger than yours.

**Ability Score Increase.** +1 Constitution  
**Brave.** You have Advantage on saving throws you make to avoid or end the Frightened condition on yourself.  
**Bend Fate.** Whenever you use your *Lucky* trait, choose a creature within 30 feet of you and grant it either Advantage or Disadvantage on the next attack roll, ability check, or saving throw it makes within 1 minute.

<ins>Meadow Halfling.</ins>  
**Ability Score Increase.** +1 Charisma  
**Naturally Stealthy.** You can take the *Hide* action even when you are obscured only by a creature that is at least one size larger than you.  
**Fated Outcome.** Whenever you use your *Lucky* trait, instead of rolling you can treat the die as if it had rolled a 10.

<ins>Thicket Halfling.</ins>
