## Basic information
#### Anatomy
Elves resemble tall, lean humanoids with long, pointed ears and big eyes lacking visible pupils. Elves seem to stop physically aging during their early adulthood. Grey hair, wrinkles and the like are unheard of in elvenkind. Elven skeletons appear to be lighter than those of other species due to partially pneumatized bones, while their joints tend to be more flexible.  
Dawn elves tend to have paler skin than their cousins, with light colored hair and eyes.  
Dusk elves tend to have darker skin than their cousins, with warm colored eyes.  
Solar elves tend to have skin with coloration similar to tree bark, with eyes and hair colored like foliage.
Lunar elves tend to have skin in cold tones, with dark eyes and hair, as well as fins.
#### Habitats
Any.
#### Diet
Omnivorous, but primarily herbivorous.
#### Growth rate
Although elves reach physical maturity in their late thirties, the elven understanding of adulthood goes beyond physical growth to encompass worldly experience. An elf typically claims adulthood around the age of 100 and can live to be 500 years old.  
## Civilization
#### History
According to ancient, elvish history, long before the current elves walked the lands, there existed a group of immortals spread far and wide through the lands of Vendria, remembered only as the True Elves. Elves of today consider themselves the descendants of said True Elves, who left Vendria for a higher calling and left the lands in the care of their children.  
The dawn elves originate from Hirana and pride themselves in being the lands' first inhabitants, often fighting with dragonborn for the title. It were the dawn elves that first saw how weak all people of their land were against the forces of nature and monstrosities lurking therein and the first to push for forming a single, unified nation.  
The dusk elves originate from Hirana, but unlike their surface cousins, they have settled in the underground caverns of the land. That is where the had met cave dwarves and where the two ancestries formed a union that expanded the underground caverns and built many powerful cities within, even before they joined the unified Kingdom of Hirana.  
The solar elves originate from the heavily forested areas of Bergov. For a while, they lead a secluded lifestyle, actively avoiding the other mortals of the land. When their dwarven neighbors began pushing into elven territories in attempts to expand their kingdom, the elves revealed themselves and began a war over borders and resources. Their conflicts came to an end when both of the nations were defeated by humans and together with them formed the Bergovian Kingdom.
The lunar elves originate from shores and small islands of Nael. They had a lucrative friendship with other mortals of the realm and had no aspirations to expand their reach beyond the few villages they lived in. 
#### Historical Organizations
- School of Shiryu-Ha - a traditional school of swordsmanship, claimed to have been developed to slay dragons with but a single stroke of a blade
- Verdant Guard - ancient order of druidic warriors and balance keepers, who guard Bergovians from fey, and the fey from Bergovians 
- Spider Lotus Triad - a criminal organization with roots in the Spider Clan, some claim they cooperate and still serve the Lordship
#### Common Taboos
- Romantic relationships with non-elves
- Badly maintained physical appearance (Dawn and Dusk)
- Ostentatious fashion and wearing precious metals/gemstones (Solar and Lunar)
#### Historical figures
- First Lord Eiravel Lathas - one of the founders of the Kingdom of Hirana and its first ruler
- The Ranger - a mysterious figure, said to take on the most dangerous missions north of the Rampart
- Captain Savona Anjou d'Ailly - naval captain of the Empire and the first woman to cross the waters between the Western and Eastern continent
