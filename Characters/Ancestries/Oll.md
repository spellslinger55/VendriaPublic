## Basic information
#### Anatomy
Oll (loosely translated to "Giantblood" in the Common Language) resemble large, powerful humanoids. Despite being considered a single species, the three known lineages of oll vary greatly in appearance and external biology.  
Skjoll have rough skin in grey, stone-like shades, that is naturally resistant to the cold and sharp winds, with no hair. Some skjoll possess patches of discolored skin mostly in brown and black shades. They have noticeably bony and prominent brow ridges and calcified dermal deposits on their bodies.  
Tholl have skin in color ranging from pale purple to dim grey. All tholl have only one eye but it is not consistent in how it appears. Some have a singular, big bulging eye in the middle of their face, some have one atrophied, vestigial eye socket while the other, working eye sits on the other half of their face, while some have an eye on their forehead with skin covering where most humanoids have eye sockets.  
Vaoll are horned humanoids with bovine features, though not all of them the same. Some common vaoll features include: cloven feet; a short tail with a tuft of fur at the end; thick, long fur; flat, bovine noses; large, floppy ears. These features do not appear to be hereditary.
#### Habitats
Polar mountains, forests and deserts.
#### Diet
Omnivorous.
#### Growth rate
Oll mature at the same rate as humans and similarly to dwarves, reach adulthood at the age of 50.
## Civilization
#### History
According to the legends of the oll, they were formed by the Forefather of the Gangrandr, and left on the lands of Varholt to care for Vendria in their place, when they had left to guard the graveyard of their gods. Their initial guardianship over Varholt was short-lived, as the Vurm scourge quickly arrived in the frozen north and overpowered all its mortal inhabitants.
#### Historical Organizations
- Pathfinders - nomadic reindeer herders that act as couriers between the Tribes
- Hala's Bones - band of mercenaries operating mostly outside of Varholt, who all carve magical runes into their skin and muscles
- the Dragonrot - a cult of Vurm followers
#### Common Taboos
- Stagnation, to stay in one place too long is to die  
- Abandonment, of person, place, object  
- Vurm hexcraft (draconic magic), and curses
#### Historical figures
- Vanadi the Mosskeeper - progenitor of the Vanadi Tribe and the greatest herbalist, apothecary and poisoner to grace Varholt
- Patriot - the greatest living warrior of all the tribes, as well as, the father of the current Hakan
- Altan the Spitting Dagger - Varholt's greatest orator and skald, master of stories and undisputed ruler of flyting

**Touch of Stone.** You have Resistance to Cold damage and you’re acclimated to high altitude, including elevations above 20,000 feet.  
**Gift of the Stone.** You can focus yourself to occasionally shrug off injury. When you take damage, you can use your Reaction to roll a d12. Add your Constitution modifier to the number rolled, and reduce the damage by that total. You can use this trait a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.   

<ins>Tholl (Stormblood).</ins>

**Gift of the Storm.** You always have the *Speak with Animals* spell prepared. You can cast it without a spell slot a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest. You can also use any spell slots you have to cast the spell.
