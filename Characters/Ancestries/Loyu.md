## Basic information
#### Anatomy
Loyu resemble aquatic humanoids. Their bodies are adapted to living in and near bodies of water. These adaptations include webbed fingers, fins, powerful tails, and small, colorful scales.
Shiftingscale Loyu have iridescent scales that change in color.
Voltaicscale Loyu have conductive scales that glow with electrical charges.
#### Habitats
Any fresh-water and marine.
#### Diet
Omnivorous, but primarily carnivorous.
#### Growth rate
Loyu age faster than humans and reach adulthood around the age of 15.
## Civilization
#### History
Loyu were the last people of mainland Hirana who joined the kingdom when it was first formed. Originally content with and protective of the deep lakes and inland seas they called their home, loyu were swayed with the promise of conquering the ocean waves and became the primary sailors and admirals of the hiranian navy.
#### Historical Organizations
- Wayve - primary barge and water-travel service in mainland Hirana, the most popular mode of transport in the kingdom thanks to the extensive waterways that spread through all its lands
- Blades of Honshou - originally ritualistic duelists who settled scores between different loyu settlements without need of large scale combat, nowadays bodyguards and duelists of the Great Clans and the Lordship 
- Order of the Crimson Waste - ancient military order whose purpose was to protect the Material Plane from incursions from the Elemental Plane, and to destroy cults and servants of the Primeval Sovereigns  
#### Common Taboos
- Shaking hands, 'tis a barbarous greeting
- Public displays of affection
#### Historical figures
- Golden Teeth Vayl - one of the Five Sea Princes, captain of the Golden Grin Pirates, famed for their outlaw "chivalry", aka stealing from the rich, freeing slaves, and opposing the governments of the world
- Hye-won Minnsun - master of the Threefold Blade: bladesmith, swordmaster and chef
- the Witch of Sabun - legendary spellcaster, who supposedly cut out her own heart and sacrificed it to the southern swamps of Sabun for immortality and forgotten knowledge

# Loyu
**Ability Score Increase.** +2 Strength
**Size.** Medium
**Speed.** 30ft, 30ft swim
**Size.** Your size is Medium.  
**Amphibious.** You can breathe air and water, and have a swimming speed equal to your movement speed.  
**Electroperception.** As a bonus action, you can sense the current location of all creatures within 15 feet of you.
**Blood in the Water.** When you miss an attack roll against a creature that doesn't have all its hit points, you can reroll the attack. You can use this trait a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest.
