## Basic information
#### Anatomy
Ravgas resemble tall humanoids mutated by negative energy. Their muscles are shrunken and wound-up around their skeletons, causing ravgas to appear thin and skeletal, without losing actual muscle strength. Their skin is withered, in pale colors, and clings tightly to their frames, adding to their undead look. The most striking feature of the ravgas is their naturally generated negative energy, which is stored in their bones and manifests a cold glow, that lights up portions of their bodies from the inside out.
#### Habitats
Polar forests and deserts.
#### Diet
Omnivorous.
#### Growth rate
Ravgas mature at a slightly slower rate humans do and reach adulthood in their early twenties.
## Civilization
#### History
The birth of Ravgas is intrinsically tied to the existence of the Vurms. As these undead tyrant conquered Varholt, their necrotic energies spilled from their bodies and seeped into the land itself as well as the mortals living upon in. Many died of esoteric illnesses in the initial wave, but some who survived were forever changed, as their bodies absorbed and adapted to necrotic energy.
#### Historical Organizations
- the Ravens - archaeologists and tomb raiders, who specialize in traversing Vurm corrupted ruins and retrieving artifacts for the Tribes
- Helforged - most elite warriors of all varrian tribes, who carry on the teachings and combat techniques of Stanayit himself
- Truthseekers - scientists, apothecaries and spellcasters obsessed with removing the necrotic energies intrinsic in ravgas to return to a truer, purer form
#### Common Taboos
- Burning waste and trash
- Being unclean/filthy (it is common to carry around additional waterskins and rags for cleaning oneself)
- Vurm hexcraft (draconic magic), and curses in general
#### Historical figures
- Stanayit the Helwalker -  progenitor of the Stanayit Tribe and the greatest warrior, tracker and monster slayer to grace Varholt
- Daener Daenorrson - sage and hermit, exiled from the Almr tribe for performing forbidden magics
- Zaya Grimdottir - current champion and star of *hakush*, traditional varrian wrestling
