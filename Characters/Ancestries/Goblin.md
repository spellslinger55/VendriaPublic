## Basic information
#### Anatomy
Goblins resemble humanoids of short stature, with large, protruding ears (that they can move). Goblins tend to have skin in yellow-green shades, but though less common, goblins with other shades of skin do exist. Their eyes are small, beady and tend to have vibrant colors, like bright yellow or red. Goblin fur tends to be short, scruffy and grow thick on the end of their ears and on their forearms. Goblins have an extremely resilient set of permanent teeth.
#### Habitats
Any.
#### Diet
Omnivorous, but primarily insectivorous.
#### Growth rate
Goblins mature about twice as fast as humans do and reach adulthood in their early teens.
## Civilization
#### History
Goblins are the smallest and weakest of all goblinoids. Historically, the goblinoids zealously waged war and opposed all civilizations they had come into contact with, offering their carnage, bloodshed and destruction to their foul deity, KALAKAR.
During the conquest of Khez by the Astrian Empire, many goblins were abandoned by their larger cousins and used as bait and meat shields. Whether due to the abuse or some esoteric even, these goblins, captured by the soldiers of the Empire, lacked the blind faith prominent in all goblinoids. Albeit they were used as tools and test subjects by the Empire, with its fall the goblins found themselves free and welcomed to khezian society.
#### Historical Organizations
- The Nightmen's Guild - the guild that runs and maintains all sewer systems below Cayra
- Lil' Troupe - one of the most successful gladiatorial teams in Cayra, which employs only goblins 
- Church of Saint Uzbin the Sower - church dedicated to a goblin Saint that developed khezian agriculture and is said to have protected Khez from a looming famine
#### Common Taboos
- Following the goblinoid faith
- Direct eye contact while speaking with somebody  
- Leaving unfinished food
#### Historical figures
- Yabal - first leader and representative of goblinkind after their desertion from the greater goblinoid warhorde
- Khrez'x - druid, shaman, philosopher and speaker of the flames, considered an insane arsonist by many
- Cathria Aht - mercenary turned poster child of House Valerius's *Valiant Guard*

**Darkvision.** You can see in dim light within 60 feet of you as if it were bright light, and in darkness as if it were dim light. You can't discern color in darkness, only shades of gray.  
**Fury of the Small.** When you damage a creature with an attack or a spell and the creature's size is larger than yours, you can cause the attack or spell to deal extra damage to the creature. The extra damage equals your proficiency bonus. You can use this trait a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, regaining all expended uses when you finish a long rest, and you can use it no more than once per turn.
