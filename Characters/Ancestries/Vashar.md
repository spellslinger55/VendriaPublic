## Basic information
#### Anatomy
Vasharr (singular Vashar) resemble humanoid felines, most often covered in thick coats of fur of different lengths, colors and patterns, which are hereditary. While most are often lithe, lean and tall, vasharr resemble many different felines, with some vasharr having a larger, muscular frame and long, wild manes, some having a smaller frame and thick, cold-resistant fur, and some of the largest members of the species rarely possessing longer front limbs and oversized incisors.
#### Habitats
Subtropical and temperate steppes and deserts.
#### Diet
Omnivorous, but primarily carnivorous.
#### Growth rate
Vasharr mature at the same rate humans do and reach adulthood in their late teens.
## Civilization
#### History
Vasharr tribes were originally nomadic people, who abandoned Shayin after its original inhabitants were driven out byt the magical Maelstrom. They traveled north and east, primarily settling in the lands today known as the Plains of Fear. Once the plains became too dangerous, vasharr returned to Shayin now settled by the genasi. From them they learned the secrets of reading the arcane storms and made their living as caravans that ventured between the cities of Shayin.
#### Historical Organizations
- The Sphynx Trading Company - Shayin's biggest merchants company and primary distributor of shanese goods to the rest of the world
- Guardian Claw - guild of assassins, who masquerade as merchant caravans, circus performers and traveling minstrels
- Department of Language Theory - one of the major departments of the Free Academy of Rovlin, which focuses their research on subjects like diplomacy, politics, linguistics, literature and most importantly, the true essence of magic, Words of Power
#### Common Taboos
- Stagnation, remaining in one place for too long
- Avoiding exposure to new experiences (vasharr suffer depressives episodes when their natural curiosity is not fed)
#### Historical figures
- Shadow at the Edges of Vision - oathkeeper of Nahr, said to relentlessly pursue any and all who break divine vows 
- Strawberry Red Morning Sky - current Katan, speaker of the Cunei Caste, their representative on the political stage of Shayin, radical who wants to abolish the shanese caste system
- Mir Abderahim - legendary shanese archer, who was said to be able to fire three arrows at three different targets all from atop his galloping horse

# Vashar
**Ability Score Increase.** +1 Charisma, +1 Dexterity
**Size.** Medium
**Speed.** 35ft, 35ft climbing
**Darkvision.** You have a cat's keen senses, especially in the dark. You can see in dim light within 60 feet of you as if it were bright light, and in darkness as if it were dim light. You can't discern color in darkness, only shades of gray.  
**Cat's Claws.** Because of your claws, you have a climbing speed equal to your walking speed. In addition, you can use your claws to make unarmed strikes with. When you hit with them, you deal slashing damage equal to 1d6 + your Strength modifier, instead of the bludgeoning damage normal for an unarmed strike.  
**Nimble Grace.** When you fall and aren't incapacitated, you can subtract up to 30 feet from the fall when calculating falling damage
