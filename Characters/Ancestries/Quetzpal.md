## Basic information

#### Anatomy
Quetzpalin resemble reptilian humanoids. Their bodies are covered in tough scales, in many vibrant shades (though dark green is most common), usually with a discolored underbelly. Quetzpalin are sexually dimorphic with the males of the species being commonly larger, with broader shoulders and wider tails than their female counterparts. While primarily crocodilian in features, quetzpalin carryover many genetic mutations originating from their extinct, reptilian cousins, such as: colorful frills; hardened scales; spines; curled tails; webbing; and even feathers.
#### Habitats
Tropical forests, marshes and volcanic deserts.
#### Diet
Omnivorous, but primarily carnivorous.
#### Growth rate
Quetzpalin age faster than humans and reach adulthood at the age of 14.
## Civilization
#### History
Before the Nukomikarangi Dominion conquered the archipelago, many tribes of different reptilian mortals resided within. Quetzpalin are the mongrels and hybrids that came to be from the tribes that survived the conquest and were enslaved by the Dominion. 
#### Historical Organizations
- Claw and Fang - paramilitary organization that denounces the current peace of Qan and believes that the natural, perfect state of the archipelago is war
- Bloodeye Order - secret group working within the shadows of Qan since the time of the Dominion, supposedly pulling at the strings of all the important mortals within the archipelago 
- Sanctuary of Speakers - religious organization tasked with finding, documenting and celebrating all the Saka of Qan
#### Common Taboos
- Burying the dead
- Tail cutting
- Slavery
#### Historical figures
- Mazati - current star and reigning champion of *huitzna*, the most popular sport in all Qan
- Usk the Wounded Vengeance - legendary warrior, who when slain by his nukomikarangi slavers rose from the dead through the power of his will alone, again and again and again, until the Dominion fell
- Nelani - greatest terror lizard trainer and breeder in qanese history, whose mighty beasts are said to still be bounded to them even years after death

# Quetzpal
**Ability Score Increase.** +2 Constitution
**Size.** Medium
**Speed.** 30ft, swim 30ft.
**Semiaquatic.** You can hold your breath for up to 1 hour at a time, and have a swimming speed equal to your movement speed.
**Bite.** You have a fanged maw that you can use to make unarmed strikes. When you hit with it, you deal piercing damage equal to 1d6 + your Strength modifier, instead of the bludgeoning damage normal for an unarmed strike.  
**Hunter's Lore.** You have proficiency with two of the following skills of your choice: Animal Handling, Nature, Perception, Stealth, and Survival.  
**Natural Armor.** You have tough, scaly skin. When you aren't wearing armor, your AC is 12 + your Constitution modifier. You can use your natural armor to determine your AC if the armor you wear would leave you with a lower AC. A shield's benefits apply as normal while you use your natural armor.
