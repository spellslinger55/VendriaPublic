The Pantheon originates from the Archipelago of Qan. Unlike other religious groups, the followers of this Pantheon do not refer to their divine beings as Gods, instead using the original Qanese word: Saka, which is roughly translated as avatar, incarnation, idea. God or Deity can and is used in Common, but traditionally, Saka is more accurate and encompasses their beings better.
The Saka are elemental spirits and personifications of the world and concepts originating from it, who take the forms of animals. Unlike the other Pantheons, there are more Saka than the members of their Pantheon, as many Saka are not benevolent, instead bringing misfortune and curses upon the people they meet. Some, while not malevolent, are also weaker than members of the Pantheon and not celebrated as much. Only six Saka are considered powerful and benevolent enough to be part of the Pantheon. Despite all that, it is not rare to hear of people praying to and following lesser, minor Sakas.
The Old Qanese Pantheon consisted of 108 sakas.
