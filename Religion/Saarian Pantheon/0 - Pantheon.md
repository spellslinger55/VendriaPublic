The Pantheon originates from the Saarian continent, and is most popular in the regions of Hirana and Shayin. While the core beliefs of the religion are the same in both regions, many myths and philosophies differ in the different branches, even within the nations themselves.  
The nine Saarian gods are believed to be the Major Constellations seen in the night sky. Those star clusters are believed to be the true physical bodies of said deities. There also exist eighteen Minor Constellations, two assigned to each Major Constellation, according to their positions in the Heavens. These constellations are epithets of the gods, which different sects interpret differently.  
The Sun and the Moon, despite being great celestial bodies just like the constellations, are not praised or celebrated, for they are the prisons of the immortal Twin Beasts, the enemies of gods and people, who plan to escape their restraints and destroy the worlds.
